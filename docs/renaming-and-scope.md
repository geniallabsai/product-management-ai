# Renaming and Scope Expansion

The project started as Product Management AI. The broader and more accurate name is now **Startup AI OS**.

## Why the change

Product Management is one critical layer, but the intended product is bigger: it should help create and improve entire companies.

## New promise

Startup AI OS helps founders, operators, PMs and technical teams build, audit, digitize and scale companies using the operating model of a modern technology company.

## Compatibility

- New command: `startup-ai`
- Alias kept: `pm-ai`
- Old PM concepts remain as a product layer inside the larger OS.

## Scope

- Business model and value proposition.
- Product and MVP.
- Engineering and architecture.
- Data, AI and MLOps.
- DevOps and platform readiness.
- Growth, sales and marketing.
- Operations and transformation of traditional companies.
